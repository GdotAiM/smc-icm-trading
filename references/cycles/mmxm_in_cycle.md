## The Four Steps of MMXM

### Step 1: Identify the HTF Point of Interest (POI)
- Must be on Daily or 4H timeframe
- POI = unmitigated Order Block or significant FVG
- This is where institutions have unfilled orders
- The POI must be at a premium (sell model) or discount (buy model)
- PD Array confirmation: price at premium for shorts, discount for longs

### Step 2: Wait for the Inducement (Manipulation)
- Price must sweep a liquidity pool BEYOND the POI
- For sell model: BSL sweep above the OB (traps breakout buyers)
- For buy model: SSL sweep below the OB (traps breakdown sellers)
- This is the " Judas Swing" — the false move that traps retail
- The sweep must reverse — candle closes back on the original side

### Step 3: Enter at the Return to POI
- After the sweep reverses, price returns to the OB/FVG zone
- Entry at the OTE (Optimal Trade Entry) = 62-79% retracement of the displacement leg
- Confirmation: FVG fill + displacement resume OR MSS on LTF
- This is where OTE + OB, Unicorn, and SCOB fit as entry refinements

### Step 4: Manage Through Distribution
- Price expands in the direction of the larger trend
- Trail SL behind each new swing
- TP1 at nearest opposing liquidity pool
- TP2 at the next major PD array or measured move
- The distribution phase IS the profitable part of the trade
