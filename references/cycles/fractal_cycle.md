## The Trading Implication

You NEVER trade against the HTF cycle. If the daily is in DISTRIBUTION (bearish):

- 15m ACCUMULATION: WAIT — price is ranging, no entry yet
- 15m MANIPULATION: WATCH — a BSL sweep on 15m within daily bearish = short entry opportunity
- 15m DISTRIBUTION: ENTER — 15m trending bearish aligned with daily bearish
- 15m EXPANSION: MANAGE — trail stops, don't add

**The highest-probability entry**: When TWO timeframes are in the SAME phase in the SAME direction. Example: Daily DISTRIBUTION (bearish) + 15m DISTRIBUTION (bearish) = enter on 5m trigger.

**The trap**: When LTF shows the OPPOSITE direction. A 15m bullish CHoCH during daily bearish distribution is NOT a reversal — it's a pullback within the larger trend. Entering long here is the #1 retail mistake.
