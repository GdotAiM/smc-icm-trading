## Pair Selection Rules

- Trade maximum 2 pairs simultaneously
- Do not trade correlated pairs in the same direction (e.g., short EURUSD + short GBPUSD)
- Prefer pairs where the current session aligns with the pair's typical activity
- Skip any pair approaching major news events
