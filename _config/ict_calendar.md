# ICT Calendar — Temporal Trading Rules

## Day-of-Week Profiles

ICT teaches that each day has a distinct character. Models that work on Wednesday may fail on Monday.

| Day | ICT Name | Character | Best Models | Risk Level | Notes |
|-----|----------|-----------|-------------|------------|-------|
| **Monday** | Range Set Day | Weekly range established. Often range-bound first half, manipulation second half. | Asian Range, NWOG/NDOG, Judas Swing | Low — wait for range to set | Don't trade first 2h of London. Let the weekly range establish. |
| **Tuesday** | Continuation Day | Monday's range extends or reverses. "Turnaround Tuesday" — watch for Monday high/low sweep then reverse. | Breaker Block, OTE + OB, Silver Bullet | Medium | If Monday was range-bound, Tuesday is the expansion day. |
| **Wednesday** | Reversal Day | Classic ICT reversal. Often marks the weekly high or low. NY AM session critical. | ★ Turtle Soup, Judas Swing, Silver Bullet (NY AM) | Medium-High | Highest probability reversal day. Watch for manipulation then expansion. |
| **Thursday** | Expansion Day | Strongest trending day. Post-reversal continuation. Institutions distribute/accumulate aggressively. | ★ MMXM (primary), Unicorn, SCOB, 2FVG, OTE + OB | High | Best day for trend trades. Highest win rate for MMXM. |
| **Friday** | Position Squaring | Profit-taking dominates. Thursday's move often retraces. Low volume in PM. | Quick scalps only (Silver Bullet AM) | Low | Close all positions by NY close. No new swing trades. No weekend holds. |

## Day Weight Multipliers

Apply these to model confidence scores based on the current day:

| Day | Multiplier | Reason |
|-----|-----------|--------|
| Monday | ×0.8 | Lower conviction — weekly range not yet established |
| Tuesday | ×1.0 | Neutral — range extending or reversing |
| Wednesday | ×1.2 | Higher conviction — classic reversal patterns reliable |
| Thursday | ×1.3 | Highest conviction — strongest trending day |
| Friday | ×0.6 | Low conviction — position squaring distorts signals |

## Weekly Cycle Position

Where we are in the week matters as much as the day:

| Period | Character | Models |
|--------|-----------|--------|
| **Monday AM** | Gap analysis. Where did we open vs Friday close? | NWOG if gap exists |
| **Monday PM** | Weekly range setting. High/low of the week often forms Mon PM - Tue AM. | Asian Range |
| **Tuesday PM - Wednesday AM** | Weekly reversal zone. If Monday trended, expect reversal. | Turtle Soup, Breaker |
| **Wednesday PM - Thursday** | Weekly expansion. The big move of the week. | ★ MMXM, 2FVG, SCOB |
| **Friday AM** | Late continuation or early squaring. Take what's given. | Silver Bullet (AM only) |
| **Friday PM** | Close all positions. No new trades. Journal the week. | — |

## Monthly Events

| Event | Week | Impact | Action |
|-------|------|--------|--------|
| **NFP (Non-Farm Payrolls)** | 1st Friday | Extreme volatility | No positions 30min before/after. Let the spike settle. |
| **FOMC Rate Decision** | Every 6 weeks, Wednesday | Extreme — can reverse any trend | Reduce size 50%. Wait for statement before entering. |
| **CPI / Inflation Data** | 2nd week, Tue-Thu | High volatility | Tighten SL, reduce size |
| **Options Expiry** | 3rd Friday | Distortion — pinning action | Expect range-bound, avoid breakouts |
| **Month-End Rebalancing** | Last 2-3 trading days | Institutional flow — can reverse technicals | Follow the flow, not the structure |
| **Quarter-End** | Mar/Jun/Sep/Dec last days | Major rebalancing | Expect unusual correlations |

## Holiday Impact

| Holiday | Pairs Affected | Action |
|---------|---------------|--------|
| **US Bank Holiday** | All USD pairs | Low volume, avoid USD. Trade crosses instead. |
| **UK Bank Holiday** | GBP pairs | Low GBP volume. Avoid GBPUSD, EURGBP. |
| **Christmas/New Year** | All pairs | Thin liquidity. Size down 75% or stay out. |
