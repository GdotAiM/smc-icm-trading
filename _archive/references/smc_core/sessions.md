## Killzone Windows

### London Killzone (07:00-10:00 UTC)
- **Character**: Institutional manipulation. Often a false move first,
  then the real direction.
- **Best Pairs**: EURUSD, GBPUSD, EURGBP (European pairs)
- **Pattern**: Judas Swing in first hour → reversal into real move

### NY AM Killzone (12:00-15:00 UTC)
- **Character**: Highest volume session. Displacement-heavy.
  Continuation or reversal of London's direction.
- **Best Pairs**: All USD pairs, XAUUSD
- **Pattern**: London direction often continues into first hour,
  then NY establishes its own direction

### NY PM Killzone (17:00-20:00 UTC)
- **Character**: Lower volume. Late continuation or reversal.
  Often ranges after the initial move.
- **Best Pairs**: USDJPY, USDCAD
- **Pattern**: First 2 hours have the most movement

### Asian Session (00:00-07:00 UTC)
- **Character**: Range-bound. Accumulation for the next session.
- **Caution**: Most Asian range breakouts are false. Wait for London.
- **Exception**: USDJPY and AUDUSD can trend during Asia

## Silver Bullet Windows

ICT's highest-probability time windows for the Silver Bullet entry model:

| Window | UTC Time | Duration | Best For |
|--------|----------|----------|----------|
| London SB | 08:00-10:00 | 2 hours | EURUSD, GBPUSD |
| NY AM SB | 13:00-15:00 | 2 hours | All USD majors |
| NY PM SB | 17:00-19:00 | 2 hours | USDJPY |

Silver Bullet criteria:
- Clear HTF bias established
- Displacement during the SB window
- FVG forms during displacement
- Entry on FVG fill + confirmation
